Fluffy Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Instructions

  1. Mix the dry ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Add the egg, milk and butter and stir until smooth.
  3. Pour some batter onto a hot pan.
  4. Flip when you see bubbles, cook the other side.
  5. Serve with syrup and enjoy!

Easy Tomato Pasta

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 1 can of tomato sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Parmesan cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Boil the spaghetti until it is soft, then drain it.
  2. Heat the olive oil and cook the garlic for a minute.
  3. Add the tomato sauce, salt and pepper.
  4. Mix the pasta into the sauce.
  5. Top with cheese and serve.

Fresh Garden Salad

Ingredients

  • Lettuce
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 cucumber
  • Half an onion
  • Olive oil and lemon juice
  • Salt

Instructions

  1. Wash and chop all the vegetables.
  2. Put everything in a big bowl.
  3. Drizzle olive oil and lemon juice on top.
  4. Add a little salt and mix well.
  5. Serve fresh.

Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • Half cup butter
  • Half cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup chocolate chips
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla

Instructions

  1. Mix the butter and sugar together.
  2. Add the egg and vanilla.
  3. Stir in the flour and chocolate chips.
  4. Scoop onto a tray and bake at 350°F for about 10 minutes.
  5. Let them cool and enjoy.
